The separate phase diagnostic is excluded from primary TPOT.

For each dataset and local batch 8, 16, 64, use exactly 128 input tokens and 32 decode forwards (33 output tokens including the prefill result). Two rank-batch/sample seed combinations are nominated from a bounded 24 sample seeds × 8 rank-order seeds search. Existing variable-context routing is only a ShareGPT screening proxy; it is neither the physical 128-token route nor an exact TPOT oracle. Report all measured candidates, then choose the largest observed CA_NATIVE-vs-BR and Near-vs-BR TPOT gaps separately. Never call the bounded winner a global maximum. Freeze target and disjoint warmup requests; do not use a measured policy to choose its own routing.

Measure primary TPOT by the maximum rank completion timestamp for each boundary, over the 32 decode forwards. It includes routing, metadata, H2D dependencies, dispatch, expert execution, return, and combine, but excludes attention/dense residual. Diagnostic instrumentation perturbs timing, so its MoE number is not subtracted from clean TPOT.
